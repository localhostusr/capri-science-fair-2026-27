💡 Project Idea Starters

What do you enjoy most? Pick your favorite thing, find the science in it, and make it your own.

⚽ Sports & Movement

  • Does a cold basketball bounce lower than a warm one?
  • Which soccer kick sends the ball the farthest?
  • Does stretching help you jump higher?
  • Why does a curveball curve?

🍳 Food & Cooking

  • What makes popcorn pop — and which brand pops best?
  • Why do cookies spread in the oven?
  • Which fruit browns the fastest once it's cut?
  • Does salt make ice melt faster?

🌿 Nature & Animals

  • Do ants prefer sugar or protein?
  • Which bird seed attracts the most birds?
  • Do plants grow toward light or toward water?
  • What's living in a drop of pond water?

🎵 Music & Sound

  • Do plants grow faster with music?
  • How does water level change the note a glass makes?
  • Can you hear sound underwater?
  • Does music change your heart rate?

🧱 Games, Toys & Lego

  • Can a Lego bridge hold a backpack?
  • Which yo-yo spins the longest, and why?
  • Does a toy car go farther down a steeper ramp?
  • Is a dice roll really random? Roll it 100 times!

🎢 Rides & Motion

  • Why do roller coasters make your stomach drop?
  • Can a marble roller coaster do a loop?
  • Which paper airplane flies the farthest?
  • How does a parachute's size change how fast it falls?

🚀 Space & Weather

  • Can you predict tomorrow's weather by looking at clouds?
  • Build a rain gauge — how much rain fell this week?
  • Why does the moon look bigger near the horizon?
  • Which color absorbs the most heat from the sun?

🫀 Body & Health

  • Does hand sanitizer work better than soap?
  • How does sleep affect memory?
  • Which sunscreen blocks the most sun?
  • Does exercise change how fast your heart recovers?

🏗️ Building & Engineering

  • What bridge design holds the most weight?
  • Can you build a tower using only paper and tape?
  • Which boat design holds the most pennies before sinking?
  • Can you build a solar oven that melts chocolate?

✨ Something Else

  • Which material keeps an ice cube frozen the longest?
  • Does the shape of a bubble wand change the bubble?
  • Which paper towel brand is the most absorbent?
  • Got your own idea? Ask your teacher about it!

📚 All resources are free and from official sources. Capri Elementary PTA does not endorse any specific program — these are starting points for your own exploration.
