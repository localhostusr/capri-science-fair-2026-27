Science Fair

“Science in Life!”
Thursday, November 19•5–7 PM•MPR

What do you enjoy most? Is it sports, food, nature, music, games, Lego, Disneyland, or anything else that interests you? Find the science in it!

  • Open to all students in grades K–6.
  • Ask your teacher, the STEM teacher, your friends, and your parents about your ideas.
  • Students can work alone or in groups of up to three.
  • Projects should answer a scientific question about something that interests you.
  • Parents will receive the Science Fair Guide with information on how students can design and display a project and manage the timeline.
  • A quantity of tri-fold presentation boards will be available in the office, while supplies last.
